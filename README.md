# Simple Interest Calculator

A simple program that calculates simple interest.

## Formula
Simple Interest = (Principal × Rate × Time) / 100

- **Principal (P):** the initial amount of money
- **Rate (R):** annual interest rate in percent
- **Time (T):** time period in years

## Example
Principal = 1000, Rate = 5%, Time = 2 years
Simple Interest = (1000 × 5 × 2) / 100 = **100**
Total Amount = 1000 + 100 = **1100**

## How to Use
1. Enter the principal amount.
2. Enter the annual interest rate.
3. Enter the time in years.
4. The program displays the simple interest and total amount.
